# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a professional, respectful environment for everyone interested in the Persistent Cognitive Substrate (PCS) architecture, regardless of background, experience level, or affiliation.

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**

- Professional and respectful communication
- Constructive technical discussion and critique
- Focus on architectural merit and empirical evidence
- Recognition that reasonable people may disagree on technical approaches
- Respect for intellectual property and proper attribution

**Unacceptable behavior:**

- Personal attacks or ad hominem arguments
- Harassment, discrimination, or exclusionary conduct
- Misrepresentation of research findings or test results
- Violation of intellectual property rights
- Spam, trolling, or off-topic disruption

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions
- Technical correspondence regarding PCS
- Public forums where PCS is discussed
- Any interaction representing the PCS project

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to: **conduct@persistra.ai**

Reports will be reviewed and may result in:

- Warning
- Temporary restriction from project spaces
- Permanent ban from project spaces

We are committed to fair and transparent enforcement.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from established open source community standards, tailored for technical research collaboration.

## Questions

For questions about this Code of Conduct, contact: **research@persistra.ai**
